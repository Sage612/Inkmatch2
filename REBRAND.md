# Rebrand: InkMatch → PanelPact

## Decision
**New name: PanelPact**
**Tagline: “Don’t just match. Make a Pact.”**
**Secondary: “Forge Stories. Split Equity. Honor the Pact.”**

---

## Why InkMatch Failed the Promise

Your blueprint is *not* a matching app. It’s a **pact forge** — legally-aware, ghost-proof, equity-first.

- **Ink** is generic (ink = any art app). It says nothing about *manga/manhwa/webtoon panel craft*.
- **Match** promises a chat, not a commitment. Matches ghost. **Pacts don’t** — they have a 14-day clause, a PDF, and a completion rate watching.
- On forums you described (Reddit/Discord/Facebook), “matching” is the *problem* — high ghosting, no trust, AI spam. Re-using “match” anchors to that failure.

We needed a name that *is* the mechanism.

---

## Why PanelPact Wins

### Panel
- **The atomic unit of your craft.** One vertical strip or one manga page = one panel. Immediately signals *comic-native*, not “general freelance.”
- Lets us own language: *Panel Rate, Panel Viewers, Panel Shield, Panel Studio.*
- SEO: “panel” + “pact” captures `manga collaboration`, `webtoon collab`, `manhwa studio` without keyword stuffing.
- Format-native promise: vertical scroll **and** RTL flip live in the name.

### Pact
- **A pact is binding.** In fantasy (your genres: Isekai, Cultivation, LitRPG) a pact is blood-sealed. Here it’s PDF-sealed: 50/50 equity, IP terms, ghost clause.
- Implies **sweat equity** without saying “equity” (which scares new creators). Everyone understands “let’s make a pact.”
- Enables verbs: *Forge a Pact, Honor the Pact, Break the Pact → lose it.* Completion Rate becomes **Pact Rate** — cleaner, ownable.
- Trust: “Verified Human” + “Pact” scans as *human promise*, anti-AI.

### PanelPact Together
- 3 syllables + 3 syllables, symmetrical, memorable, says exactly what you do.
- **Alliteration without cheesiness** (hard P’s sound decisive — like a stamp).
- Visual identity: `PP` monogram → two panels sealing. Stamp/seal mark for the 50/50 PDF.
- Domain & handle ready: @PanelPact, panelpact.com / panelpact.art

---

## Alternatives Considered (and why PanelPact beat them)

| Name | Pros | Why not |
|---|---|---|
| **InkPact** | Keeps “Ink” equity, adds pact | “Ink” still generic; doesn’t say *panel craft*; limiting if you expand beyond inked art (writing, lettering) |
| **ForgeBond** | Forge = sweat, Bond = trust | Sounds like a fintech / metal shop; no comic signal |
| **DualCanvas** | Two creators, one canvas, 50/50 | “Canvas” is owned by Webtoon Canvas; confusion |
| **Pact & Panel** | Same words, softer | Longer, ampersand hurts domains/handles; PanelPact is punchier |
| **EquiInk** | Equity + Ink | Jargony, hard to say, feels like finance |

PanelPact was the only name that hits **all five** blueprint pillars at once: comic-native, pact/legal, ghost-proof, sweat-equity, human-verified.

---

## Migration Path (InkMatch → PanelPact)

1. **Keep the mission, change the promise.** Banner on every page: “InkMatch is now PanelPact — same mission, sharper promise.”
2. **Redirects:** inkmatch.* → panelpact.* (301)
3. **Copy swap:** “Match Request” → “Pact Request”, “Matching” → “Pacting”, “Match Rate” → “Pact Rate”
4. **Schema stays:** No DB migration — `MatchRequest` model keeps name internally, UI shows “Pact Request.”
5. **Legal:** Pact Template v2.1 carries forward; just re-header to PanelPact.

---

## Voice & Usage

- **Verb:** “Pact” is a verb. *“Want to pact on a shōnen pilot?”*
- **Noun:** *“A 50/50 Pact,” “a Squad Pact,” “the Pact PDF.”*
- **Never:** “Match with Aiko” → **“Pact with Aiko”**
- **Tagline lockup:** PanelPact wordmark + subline “DON’T JUST MATCH — MAKE A PACT”

---

## Logo & System (shipped in app)

- **Mark:** `PP` — two panels overlapping, forming a seal. Black ink (#0F0F12) + Pact Red (#FF3B30)
- **Paper:** #FFFCF8 (warm paper, not stark white — comic paper)
- **Type:** Black-heavy headings, precise small caps for meta (PACT RATE, VERIFIED HUMAN)

You asked for a name that better reflects what you *aim* to achieve. **PanelPact achieves it in two words:** the craft (Panel) and the commitment (Pact). Not a feed. A forge.
